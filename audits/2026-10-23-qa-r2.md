### QA-020: Password reset hangs indefinitely on "Sending…"
- **Area:** Storefront account (also staff reset, same Keystone hook)
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/account/reset
- **Steps:** Enter `qa+grocery-20261023@example.com` → "Email me a reset link".
- **Expected:** Within a few seconds, either "If an account exists for this email, a password reset link is on its way." or an error.
- **Actual:** The button stays "Sending…" with the field disabled for over 60s (tried twice). Calling the mutation directly (`mutation{ sendUserPasswordResetLink(email:"qa+…@example.com") }` on `/api/graphql`) returns nothing within 100s (curl `HTTP 000`). A customer locked out of their account can't recover it, and every attempt ties up a server request. The contact form, which sends through a different path, answers in under 5s.
- **Likely file:** `features/keystone/lib/mail.ts:19`. The `createTransport` has no `connectionTimeout`/`greetingTimeout`/`socketTimeout`, so an unreachable or blocked SMTP host (check the live `SMTP_HOST`/port; some hosts block outbound 25/465/587) stalls `sendToken` (`features/keystone/index.ts:71–79`) for nodemailer's multi-minute defaults. `features/integrations/communications/index.ts:13` already uses 15s/15s/30s; reuse that transport config. Also verify live SMTP delivery itself.
