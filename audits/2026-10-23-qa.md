## Major

### QA-001: 6 of 8 products are out of stock, so the store effectively sells two items
- **Area:** Catalog / inventory (storefront)
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/departments/produce (also `/dairy`, `/meat-seafood`, `/bakery`); https://juniper.openship.org/products
- **Steps:** Open Home → "Shop by aisle". Open Produce, Dairy & Eggs, Meat & Seafood or Bakery. Then open `/products`.
- **Expected:** A grocery store's staples (bananas, avocados, milk, yogurt, salmon, sourdough) are in stock. If an aisle card says "2 products", the shopper can buy them.
- **Actual:** Only Frozen Blueberries and Jasmine Rice can be added. Every item in Produce, Dairy & Eggs, Meat & Seafood and Bakery shows "Out of stock". Aisle cards say "2 products"/"1 product", but `/products` defaults to in-stock and shows "2 products". Home's "On the shelf today" shows only those two, leaving a mostly empty grid at desktop width.
- **Likely file:** Inventory data on live (stock levels / `InventoryLot` for the seeded products). For count consistency: `features/storefront/screens/DepartmentsPage.tsx` / `HomePage.tsx` (aisle count includes sold-out) vs `features/storefront/screens/ProductsPage.tsx:33` (defaults to `in-stock`). Needs dashboard access to confirm whether this is data or a stock-calculation bug.

### QA-002: 8.75% sales tax is charged on unprepared groceries
- **Area:** Checkout / tax
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/cart, https://juniper.openship.org/checkout
- **Steps:** Add Frozen Blueberries ($6.49) and 2 × Jasmine Rice. Open the basket.
- **Expected:** The store is at a San Francisco, CA address. California exempts most unprepared food (rice, frozen fruit, milk, produce) from sales tax, so the estimated tax on this basket should be $0.
- **Actual:** "Estimated tax $2.14" on $24.47 (8.75%). Order #7 was authorized at $14.12 including $1.14 tax on blueberries. Every shopper is overcharged and the merchant is collecting tax it shouldn't.
- **Likely file:** `features/keystone/models/StoreSettings.ts:127` (`taxRateBps` defaults to `875`) together with `features/keystone/models/Product.ts:297` (`taxRateBasisPoints` blank inherits the store rate). A grocery vertical should default food items to exempt, or make onboarding ask for a food tax rate. The seeded products should carry the exemption.

### QA-003: Intermittent React hydration error #418 in production
- **Area:** Storefront (PDP / layout)
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/products/frozen-blueberries (also seen on `/products/jasmine-rice`)
- **Steps:** In a fresh browser context at 1440px, load a product page. Repeat a few times.
- **Expected:** No hydration errors.
- **Actual:** `pageerror: Minified React error #418; args[]=HTML` on about 1 in 4 first loads (2 of 8 runs: once on load of `frozen-blueberries` with an empty basket, once on load of `jasmine-rice` with a basket). React then client-renders the tree, which flashes content and can drop early input.
- **Likely file:** A client component in the shared storefront chrome or the PDP renders time- or state-dependent text. Candidates: `features/storefront/modules/urban/UrbanSlotPicker.tsx` (uses `new Date()`), the header basket summary, `features/storefront/modules/products/components/product-actions`. Reproduce with a non-minified production build to get the mismatching node.

### QA-005: Delivery area is one ZIP code, and shoppers only learn this after filling out checkout
- **Area:** Fulfillment / checkout
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/fulfillment, https://juniper.openship.org/checkout
- **Steps:** Build a basket → Checkout → Home delivery → fill all details with postal code `10001` (or any SF ZIP other than 94107) → "Review checkout estimate".
- **Expected:** The delivery area is shown on `/fulfillment`, the home "Next available times" card, or a ZIP check before shopping. A San Francisco grocer would typically serve several ZIPs.
- **Actual:** The rejection appears only at review: "We don't deliver to postal code 10001. We deliver to postal codes starting with 94107." The live policy is a single ZIP, `/fulfillment` advertises 28 delivery times without naming an area, and "postal codes starting with 94107" is awkward wording for a full ZIP.
- **Likely file:** `features/keystone/lib/deliveryPolicy.ts:31` (message), `features/storefront/screens/FulfillmentPage.tsx` (no service-area disclosure). Live delivery-zone settings are store data and were not changed.

### QA-006: No product has a description, ingredients, allergens or nutrition
- **Area:** Catalog content (PDP)
- **URL:** https://juniper.openship.org/products/frozen-blueberries, https://juniper.openship.org/products/jasmine-rice
- **Steps:** Open any product.
- **Expected:** A grocery PDP has at least a description, pack size, and ingredients/allergens for packaged food.
